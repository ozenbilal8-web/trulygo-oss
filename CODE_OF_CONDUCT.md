# Code of Conduct

TrulyGo OSS welcomes participants of all backgrounds and experience levels.
This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, and project
spaces maintained by the repository maintainers.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people respectfully and give constructive, specific feedback.
- Discuss technical choices without attacking the people making them.
- Respect differing experience levels, identities, and communication styles.
- Respect privacy and obtain permission before sharing personal information.
- Accept a request to stop unwanted contact or disruptive behavior.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing,
impersonation, and repeated disruption are unacceptable. Good-faith technical
disagreement is welcome.

## Reporting and enforcement

For conduct concerns, contact a maintainer through a private contact method
currently published on their GitHub profile. If none is available, open a
minimal issue requesting a private contact channel, without naming affected
people or sharing incident details. GitHub's own reporting tools may also be
used for platform abuse. Do not post private reports or personal details in
public issues. For software vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers will review reports as capacity permits, protect confidentiality
where practical, and consider context and affected participants. Responses
may include a request to change behavior, removal of content, a warning,
temporary restrictions, or removal from project spaces. There is no promised
response deadline or confidential reporting infrastructure beyond the
available channels; do not assume a public issue is private.

A maintainer involved in a report should not decide that report alone. When
possible, another uninvolved maintainer should review an appeal or disputed
action. Reporters and participants must not face retaliation for a good-faith
report.
